Details, cultivation, and uses of cotton

Overview

Cotton is a soft, fluffy fiber that grows in a boll around the seeds of cotton plants. It is one of the most important crops for textiles worldwide.

Growing Conditions

  • Requires warm climate
  • Needs moderate rainfall or irrigation
  • Best grown in fertile, well-drained soil

Uses

Cotton is widely used in clothing, home furnishings, and industrial products. Its natural comfort and breathability make it highly popular.
